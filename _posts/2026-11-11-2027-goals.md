title: SEMPERLab Core Team Sets Goals and Plans for 2027

On 10 November 2026, the SEMPERLab core team met to set short-term work goals and draw up a plan for participation in scientific conferences and training programmes in 2027.\
The team also coordinated the implementation of ongoing research activities.
Work at SEMPERLab is in full swing, and we look forward to sharing new results and updates soon.
