# Welcome to SEMPERLab

We are SEMPERLab (Social, Emotion, and Personality Lab), a research group investigating the interplay between social, emotional, and motivational processes, particularly in the context of individual differences and their implications for well-being, with a strong emphasis on an evolutionary perspective.

One major line of our research examines interpersonal emotions, with a special focus on associated dominant and submissive behaviors, such as aggression and emotional crying, and their roles in establishing, maintaining, and changing social relationships and hierarchies.
These processes are closely intertwined with motivation and psychological well-being, which constitute another major direction of our research.
We want to know how certain motivations and their moderators are related to various well-being outcomes.
We investigate individual differences in these processes, seeking to understand why people differ in their emotional, motivational, and social functioning, for example,  why some people seek and achieve higher social ranks.
We study how personality traits shape these processes, and how personality traits themselves can be understood in light of these processes.
Finally, we examine how these processes relate to various aspects of psychological well-being with a special interest in clinical outcomes and outcomes in sports, organizational and academic settings.

Using correlational, experimental and ecological momentary assessment (EMA) designs, we integrate behavioral, self-report, and psychophysiological measures, including autonomic nervous system activity and hormonal responses, to clarify the underlying mechanisms.
